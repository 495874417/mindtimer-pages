Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I do not receive a reminder when a focus session ends?

Make sure notifications are allowed for the app, and check whether your device is in Silent, Do Not Disturb, or Focus mode.

Does my data sync to a server?

In the current version, data is stored locally on your device and no account login is required.
